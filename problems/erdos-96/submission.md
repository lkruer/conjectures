## Draft summary for author review

**Title:** Superlinearly many unit distances in convex position

**Result:** The manuscript describes convex point sets whose number of unit-distance pairs divided by their cardinality is arbitrarily large, claiming a negative answer to the linear-bound question.

**Proof idea:** A finite incidence construction is amplified using small commuting rotations. A strict support margin preserves convexity, and a radius perturbation separates the resulting points.

**Scope:** The claimed result concerns exact Euclidean unit distances and every vertex of the constructed convex set. The uploaded source is a single submission body; the PDF describes an additional project organized into 21 modules.

**Formalization:** The accompanying sources are available in the repository. The manuscript describes prior checks; this repository has not reproduced them yet.
