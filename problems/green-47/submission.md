## Summary for author review

**Title:** A counterexample to exact quadratic containment in Green’s Problem 47

**Result:** The manuscript proposes $A=\{2\}\cup\{q^2:q\text{ prime},\ q\equiv1\pmod8\}$ as a counterexample to that exact-containment implication.

**Scope:** Exact containment only. Both the PDF and the uploaded Lean header explicitly say this does not refute the formulation allowing finitely many exceptional elements, including Green and Harper’s Conjecture 1.7.

**Proof idea:** Finite-field moment identities obstruct rational quadratic containment, while a divergent prime series and dyadic summation give the required counting obstruction.

**Verification:** The manuscript describes prior checks. Repository reproduction has not been performed.
